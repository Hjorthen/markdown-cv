Experience
----------
**Director — Niels Bohr Institute, Copenhagen** 1920-1962      
Founded and directed one of the world's foremost research institutes. Recruited and hosted virtually every significant physicist of the 20th century, many of whom went on to win Nobel Prizes.

**Professor of Physics — University of Copenhagen** 1916-1920      
Tenured full professor. Delivered lectures widely described as "profound," "illuminating," and "occasionally comprehensible." Introduced the correspondence principle. Successfully modelled the hydrogen atom using quantised electron orbits, which turned out to be correct enough to win a Nobel Prize.

**Research Physicist — Victoria University of Manchester** 1912-1916      
Collaborated with Rutherford following his nuclear model of the atom. Developed the Bohr model of the atom (1913), reconciling quantum theory with atomic stability. Rutherford reportedly called it "a very interesting paper" — high praise by any account.

Projects
--------
**Senior Consultant (Classified) — Manhattan Project** 1943-1945      
Provided senior scientific advisory support to Allied nuclear research after escaping German-occupied Denmark via fishing boat. The project eventually lead to world peace.
